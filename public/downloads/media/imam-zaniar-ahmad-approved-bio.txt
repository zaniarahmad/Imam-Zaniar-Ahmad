APPROVED BIO — IMAM ZANIAR AHMAD

Imam Zaniar Ahmad is a Canadian Islamic speaker, registered Ontario marriage officiant, fundraiser, and community technology entrepreneur. He studied Arabic and Islamic Studies at the University of Madinah and Quran and Chaplaincy at the College of Masjid Nabawi, and holds an MBA in Entrepreneurship. He performs Nikah ceremonies across Ontario, delivers khutbahs, lectures, and workshops, and supports Muslim organizations through fundraising. He is also a co-founder of Zakah.com, Meezan, and AutoAjr—initiatives that make Islamic services, Zakah guidance, and charitable giving easier to access. His approach is clear, practical, and light-hearted.

Approved for event pages, speaker introductions, programs, articles, and media coverage.
